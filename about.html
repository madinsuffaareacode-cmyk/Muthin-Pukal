About Muthin Pukal

A beautiful collection of Islamic Madh Songs

Who We Are

Muthin Pukal is an online collection of Islamic Madh songs. Our platform brings together beautiful songs and praises of Prophet Muhammad ﷺ in different languages.

Our Mission

Our mission is to preserve and share Islamic Madh songs with everyone. We aim to make lyrics easily accessible in different languages so that everyone can read, learn and enjoy them.

Our Vision

"Keeping the beauty of Madh alive, one song at a time."

Explore With Us

Discover songs in Malayalam, Kannada, Arabic, Urdu and other languages. Explore our collections and enjoy the beauty of Islamic songs.
