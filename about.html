About Muthin Pukal

Who We Are

Muthin Pukal is a digital platform dedicated to Islamic Madh songs and praises of Prophet Muhammad ﷺ. We bring together beautiful lyrics from different traditions and languages in one place.

Our Mission

Our mission is to preserve, collect and share Islamic Madh songs. We aim to make lyrics easily accessible so that everyone can read, learn and share them.
